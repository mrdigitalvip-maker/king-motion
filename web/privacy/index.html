Privacy

King Motion is in development. This page describes the privacy principles guiding the product and will be updated with the complete policy before public release.

Local-first by design

The editor is being designed around processing projects on your device. Our aim is to minimize unnecessary cloud transfers and keep creative work under the creator's control.

Website data

This landing page does not provide accounts, uploads, advertising, or behavioral profiling. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request information needed to securely deliver the site.
